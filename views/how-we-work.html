The Process

How every engagement works.

Whether it's a small automation or a full ERP, every project follows the same proven path.

2

Discovery & Proposal

3

Launch & Support

We deploy, train your team, and provide ongoing support. You get direct access to the engineers who built your system.

Our Principles

Fixed pricing, not hourly

You know the cost before we start. No surprises, no scope creep fees, no "that wasn't in the original brief."

Your code, your data, your infrastructure. When the project is done, you have full ownership of everything we build.
