Questions buyers ask

What companies ask before they hire us.

Who builds AI systems for companies in Kenya?

Cres Dynamics Ltd, based at Kivuli Tower in Westlands, Nairobi, builds AI systems for companies in Kenya and beyond. These include AI agents, AI forecasting that predicts the next 3 and 6 months, AI advisors for directors, and logistics systems that predict stock, spoilage, and when to discount.

Can AI predict my sales, stock, and cash flow 3 to 6 months ahead?

Yes. We train forecasting models on your own sales, stock, and finance records to predict demand, cash position, and market shifts 3 and 6 months ahead. We already run 6-month demand forecasting in production for FMCG distribution.

What is a business operating system?

A business operating system brings finance, CRM, HR, projects, inventory, and reporting together in one platform, with AI that flags what directors need to act on. CresOS and our custom BOS platforms replace the five to ten disconnected tools most growing companies use.

Do you build school management systems?

Yes. Our school management system covers M-Pesa fee collection, admissions, CBC report cards, attendance, timetables, a parent portal, and AI insights for school owners and boards.

Do you build high-end and ecommerce websites in Kenya?

Yes. We build premium, fast, search-optimised websites and ecommerce stores with M-Pesa and card checkout, inventory, delivery tracking, and WhatsApp ordering.

Do you work with companies outside Kenya?

Yes. Systems we have built are in production in more than 10 countries across Africa and in the United States.
