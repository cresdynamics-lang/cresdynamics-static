Process

From audit to rollout.

We do not start with a tool shopping list. We start with how work moves on a busy Tuesday.

1. Systems audit

Map sales, money, people, and fulfilment loops. Identify what breaks first and what a first release must own.

2. Design the operating layer

Roles, approvals, data model, and integrations (including M-Pesa and WhatsApp where they belong).

3. Build and pilot

Ship a focused release with one team and one success metric. Keep a human override on AI paths.

4. Harden and administer

Access, backups, monitoring, and runbooks. Then expand loops once the first one is trusted.
