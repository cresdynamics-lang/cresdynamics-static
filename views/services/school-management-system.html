Questions

School management system: common questions.

Does it support M-Pesa fee payments?

Yes. Parents pay through your M-Pesa paybill, and each payment is matched to the learner's account automatically and receipted.

Does it handle CBC assessment and report cards?

Yes. It supports competency-based assessment, and report cards are generated per learner and class, alongside exam-based grading where you still need it.

Can one system run several campuses?

Yes. Each campus runs its own fees, classes, and staff, and owners and boards get one combined view across all branches.

Can we move our existing records in?

Yes. We import learner records, fee balances, and staff data from spreadsheets or your current system during setup.
