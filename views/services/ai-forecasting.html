Questions

AI forecasting: common questions.

How accurate are 3 and 6 month forecasts?

Accuracy depends on how much clean history you have and how stable the business is. Every forecast shows a confidence range, and the models are checked against real results each month, so directors can see how reliable they are.

What data do we need to start?

Usually 12 or more months of sales, invoices, or stock records. They can come from an ERP, accounting software, a POS, M-Pesa statements, or spreadsheets. If the data is scattered, we structure it first.

Can the AI advisor work with our existing systems?

Yes. We connect to the systems you already run, or deliver it inside CresOS or a custom business operating system if you need one.

Is our company data kept private?

Yes. Your data stays in your environment with role-based access, and the advisor only answers from data each user is allowed to see. See our data and security practices.
