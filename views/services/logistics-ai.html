Questions

Logistics AI: common questions.

Can it predict stock and demand for multiple warehouses?

Yes. Forecasts run per product, per warehouse, and per branch, then roll up to a group view for management.

How does it know goods are about to go bad?

Each batch is tracked with its expiry or shelf life. The system compares that with forecast sales and alerts you when a batch will not sell in time, together with a recommended discount or transfer.

Does it work with M-Pesa and our accounting system?

Yes. Payments from M-Pesa, banks, and cards are matched automatically, and the system connects to your existing accounting or ERP.

Do you build for companies outside Kenya?

Yes. Our systems run in more than 10 countries across Africa and in the United States.
