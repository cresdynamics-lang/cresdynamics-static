AI Predictions & Intelligence

Cash Flow Forecasting

See your cash position 30, 60, 90 days ahead. AI analyzes your historical transactions, pending invoices, and seasonal patterns to predict what your bank balance will be next month.

Anomaly Detection

The system learns what "normal" looks like for your business. When something unusual happens, a spike in expenses, a drop in revenue, an overdue payment, you get alerted before it becomes a problem.

Demand Prediction

AI analyzes sales trends, seasonality, and external factors to forecast demand. Know which products will sell next month, which services will be booked, and where to allocate resources.

Automated Finance

Automated Invoicing & Payments

Generate invoices automatically from project milestones, recurring billing, or triggered events. Send payment reminders, process M-Pesa and card payments, and reconcile transactions without manual entry.

Expense Automation

Receipt scanning, expense categorization, approval workflows, and budget tracking. Every shilling is accounted for without anyone touching a spreadsheet.

Real-Time Financial Dashboards

Revenue, expenses, profit margins, cash flow, all updated in real-time. See exactly where your money is, where it's going, and what your position is without waiting for month-end reports.

CRM & Client Intelligence

Smart Lead Scoring

AI scores your leads based on engagement, fit, and behavior. Your sales team focuses on the leads most likely to close, not cold contacts that will never buy.

Automated Client Communication

Welcome sequences, follow-up reminders, payment reminders, and re-engagement campaigns. Every client gets the right message at the right time, automatically.

Deal Pipeline & Forecasting

Track every deal from first contact to closed-won. AI predicts which deals will close this month and flags deals that are stalling. Your pipeline becomes predictable.

Multi-Business Unit Management

One System, Multiple Entities

Run multiple businesses, branches, or divisions from one platform. Separate financials, shared resources, consolidated reporting. See each entity individually or the whole group at once.

Cross-Entity Analytics

Compare performance across branches, regions, or business units. Identify which entities are profitable, which are growing, and where to invest or cut back.

Centralized Operations

Procurement, approvals, and HR policies applied across all entities. One approval workflow, one system of record, one source of truth for the entire business group.

Automation & Workflows

Automated Email & Notification Engine

Triggered emails for payments, approvals, reminders, onboarding, and follow-ups. Every client and team member gets the right communication at the right time, without anyone手动 sending it.

Approval Workflows

Purchase orders, expenses, leave requests, budget approvals, all routed automatically based on your rules. No more WhatsApp "boss, can you approve?" messages.

AI Decision Support

When a decision needs data, the system provides it. Should we hire another salesperson? Can we afford this expansion? Which client should we prioritize? AI surfaces the answers from your data.
