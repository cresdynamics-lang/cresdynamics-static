Case Study

Ongoza Cyber Hub

Multi-country cybersecurity learning platform serving 10,000+ students across 8+ African countries, with mentor matching, certification tracking, and regional intelligence.

EdTech Cybersecurity Multi-Country
The Challenge

10,000 Students, No Central Platform

Cybersecurity talent across Africa was growing, but there was no unified platform to connect students with mentors, track progress, or certify skills at scale.

Fragmented Learning

Students across 8+ countries were learning through scattered WhatsApp groups, PDF guides, and ad-hoc mentoring with no structured curriculum or progress tracking.

No Mentor Matching

Experienced cybersecurity professionals wanted to mentor but had no system to match them with students based on skill level, specialisation, or timezone.

No Certification System

Students completed learning paths but had no verifiable certification. Employers had no way to validate skills or assess readiness for roles.

The Solution

What Was Built

A complete cybersecurity education ecosystem, from student onboarding to certification and employer matching, built for multi-country scale.

Student Management System

Full student lifecycle from enrollment to certification, progress tracking across learning paths, skill assessments, and automated notifications for milestones.

AI Mentor Matching

Matches students with mentors based on specialisation, skill level, timezone, and learning goals. Automated scheduling and session tracking.

Certification Engine

Verifiable digital certifications based on completed learning paths and assessed competencies. Shareable on LinkedIn, verifiable by employers.

Regional Intelligence Dashboard

Real-time view of student distribution across countries, skill gaps by region, mentor availability, and learning completion rates by country.
