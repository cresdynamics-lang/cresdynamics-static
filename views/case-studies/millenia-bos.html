Case Study

Millenia BOS

Business Operating System managing 6 business units (ICT, power, wax, energy, networking) with per-unit dashboards, consolidated reporting, and cross-unit resource allocation.

BOS Multi-Business Unit Enterprise
The Challenge

6 Business Units, No Unified View

Millenia Group operates across 6 distinct business verticals. Each ran on separate tools, spreadsheets, and siloed data, making consolidated reporting impossible.

Siloed Operations

Each business unit had its own tools, spreadsheets, and reporting. No cross-unit visibility, no consolidated financial view, no shared resource planning.

Manual Consolidation

Finance team spent days each month manually consolidating data from 6 different systems into a single report. By the time data was compiled, it was already stale.

No Resource Optimisation

No visibility into how resources were distributed across units. Some units overstaffed while others were under-resourced, with no data to rebalance.

The Solution

What Was Built

A unified Business Operating System with per-unit dashboards, consolidated reporting, and cross-unit resource management, built for multi-vertical enterprises.

Per-Unit Dashboards

Each business unit has its own operational dashboard with KPIs, revenue tracking, pipeline health, and team performance, tailored to what that unit actually measures.

Consolidated Financial Engine

Real-time consolidated P&L across all 6 units, inter-company transactions, shared cost allocation, and group-level financial reporting without manual Excel work.

Cross-Unit Resource Manager

Unified view of staff, equipment, and assets across all units. Allocate resources where they're needed, track utilisation, and prevent double-booking across businesses.

Group Intelligence Dashboard

C-suite view showing performance across all units, trend analysis, risk flags, and strategic recommendations. One screen to see the entire group's health.
