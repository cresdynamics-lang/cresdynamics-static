Case Study

Manufacturing Digital Transformation

Digital operations overhaul for a manufacturing company, real-time production tracking, inventory automation, and business intelligence.

Manufacturing Ops Real-Time Tracking BI Dashboard
The Challenge

Flying Blind on the Factory Floor

The manufacturing company had no visibility into production status, inventory levels, or equipment performance, decisions were made on gut feel and delayed reports.

No Production Visibility

Production status was tracked on whiteboards and paper logs. No real-time view of what was being made, what was delayed, or what was finished.

Inventory Chaos

Raw materials and finished goods tracked manually. Frequent stockouts, over-ordering, and production delays due to missing materials.

Delayed Decision Making

Financial and operational data compiled manually in Excel, always days or weeks old. Management couldn't respond quickly to problems or opportunities.

The Solution

What CRES Built

A connected manufacturing platform, from shop floor to boardroom, with real-time data driving every decision.

Production Tracking System

Real-time production monitoring, job status, machine utilization, output tracking, and bottleneck identification. Workers log progress via tablets on the floor.

Smart Inventory Management

Barcode/QR scanning for materials and products. Automated reorder points, supplier management, and waste tracking. Production auto-consumes inventory as jobs complete.

Business Intelligence Dashboard

Real-time dashboards showing production KPIs, inventory health, financial metrics, and trend analysis. Custom alerts for deviations from plan.

Quality & Compliance Module

Digital quality checks at every production stage. Defect tracking, root cause analysis, and compliance reporting, ensuring consistent quality standards.
