Case Study

Wazaverie Fashion

AI-powered sales analytics and WhatsApp inventory alerts for a fashion business that could finally see which products were actually selling.

Retail Sales Analytics AI
The Challenge

Sales, But No Sight

Products moved, stock piled up, and cash sat tied in the wrong items. The owner could not answer the simplest question: what is actually selling?

No Product Visibility

Best sellers and dead stock were discovered too late, after money was already sunk in inventory.

Stockouts and Overstock

Popular sizes ran out quietly while slow-moving items gathered dust in the same week.

Decisions on Guesswork

Restocking, pricing, and promotions were driven by memory, not data. Every season repeated the same mistakes.

The Solution

What Was Built

A sales analytics layer on top of the business, with alerts that arrive where the owner actually lives: WhatsApp.

AI Sales Analytics

Real-time dashboards rank products by sales velocity, margin, and trend, so buying decisions follow the numbers.

WhatsApp Inventory Alerts

The system messages the owner the moment a fast mover crosses a reorder point or a size thins out.

Dead Stock Detection

AI flags items below healthy sell-through, prompting markdowns before cash is locked up for months.

Trend Forecasting

Seasonal patterns from sales history inform the next buying cycle, so restocks match demand.
