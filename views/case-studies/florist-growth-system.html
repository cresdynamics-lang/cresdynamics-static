Case Study

Florist Growth System

Automated growth and marketing system designed specifically for florists, from lead capture to repeat customer retention.

Growth Automation Marketing System CRM
The Challenge

Florists Were Growing, But Not Scaling

Individual florists and small flower shops had no system to capture leads, nurture relationships, or drive repeat business, growth was entirely word-of-mouth.

No Lead Capture

Potential customers visited websites or social pages but had no way to be captured as leads. Every visitor that didn't buy immediately was lost forever.

No Follow-Up System

No automated follow-ups for abandoned carts, no birthday reminders, no seasonal campaign automation, all follow-up was manual and inconsistent.

Customer Retention Gap

No loyalty programs, no personalized recommendations, no data on customer preferences, repeat business was accidental, not systematic.

The Solution

What CRES Built

A complete growth engine for florists, from lead capture to lifetime value optimization, all automated and data-driven.

Lead Capture & CRM

Website widgets, WhatsApp integration, and social media lead forms that automatically capture and qualify leads. Full customer profiles with order history and preferences.

Automated Marketing Campaigns

Pre-built campaign templates for Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, weddings, and corporate events. Set it once, run it every season with automated email and WhatsApp sequences.

Customer Retention Engine

Automated birthday reminders, anniversary alerts, loyalty points, and personalized product recommendations that drive repeat purchases and increase customer lifetime value.

Growth Analytics

Dashboard showing lead sources, conversion rates, campaign performance, customer segments, and revenue attribution, giving florists data to make smart growth decisions.
