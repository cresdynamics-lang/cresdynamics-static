Case Study

SACCO Financial Services

End-to-end financial management system for a SACCO serving thousands of members, from savings to loans to digital banking.

Financial System M-Pesa Integration Member Portal
The Challenge

Legacy Systems Failing Members

The SACCO was running on outdated software that couldn't handle growing membership, complex loan products, or modern digital expectations from members.

Slow Loan Processing

Loan applications took weeks to process, manual paperwork, physical approvals, and no automated credit scoring or risk assessment.

Member Frustration

Members had to visit the office for every transaction, balance checks, statements, loan applications, and dividend claims all required physical presence.

Compliance Risk

Manual reporting to regulators, no audit trail, and inconsistent data across systems created compliance risks and audit findings.

The Solution

What CRES Built

A modern financial management platform, from member self-service to automated loan processing, built for SACCO compliance and growth.

Member Self-Service Portal

Members can check balances, view statements, apply for loans, track applications, claim dividends, and update personal details, all from their phone.

Automated Loan Processing

Digital loan applications with automated credit scoring, risk assessment, approval workflows, disbursement tracking, and repayment scheduling.

M-Pesa & Payment Integration

Savings contributions, loan repayments, and fees all payable via M-Pesa. Real-time reconciliation and automated receipts sent via SMS.

Compliance & Reporting

Automated regulatory reporting, full audit trails, member communication templates, and dashboard analytics for the board and management.
