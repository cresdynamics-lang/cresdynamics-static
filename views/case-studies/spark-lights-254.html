Case Study

Spark Lights 254

Digital transformation for a lighting company, from manual processes to automated operations and online sales.

Digital Transformation E-Commerce Automation
The Challenge

Manual Operations Holding Back Growth

Spark Lights 254 had built a reputation for quality lighting solutions but their internal operations were stuck in the past, paper-based, slow, and unscalable.

Paper-Based Quoting

Quotes were created manually in Word documents, emailed as PDFs, and had to be manually followed up on, leading to lost deals and slow turnaround.

No Project Tracking

No visibility into project status, install schedules, or team assignments. Projects were managed through WhatsApp groups and phone calls.

Missing Online Presence

No e-commerce or online catalog. Customers had to visit the showroom or call to get product information and pricing.

The Solution

What CRES Built

A complete digital ecosystem, from online store to project management, designed for how a lighting company actually operates.

Product Catalog & E-Commerce

Full product catalog with specs, pricing, availability, and online ordering. Customers can browse, compare, and purchase, or request quotes for bulk orders.

Quoting & Invoicing System

Digital quoting engine, generate professional quotes in minutes, track sent quotes, automate follow-ups, and convert quotes to invoices with one click.

Project Management

Track projects from quote to installation, task assignments, progress tracking, schedule management, and client communication all in one place.

Client Portal

Dedicated client portal where customers can view their orders, track project progress, download invoices, and communicate directly with the team.
