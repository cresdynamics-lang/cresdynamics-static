OPTIOHIRE

AI-powered hiring agent that scans every applicant, matches skills to roles, and ranks candidates by market demand, eliminating bias and guesswork from recruitment.

AI Agent Talent Acquisition Machine Learning
The Challenge

Recruitment Was Slow, Biased, and Unscalable

Businesses across Africa spend weeks screening CVs, conducting unstructured interviews, and making hiring decisions based on gut feel, not data.

Manual CV Screening

Recruiters spend 23 hours per hire on average reading CVs, most of which are irrelevant. Top candidates get lost in the pile.

Unconscious Bias

Hiring managers favour names, universities, and backgrounds they recognise, systematically excluding qualified candidates from non-traditional paths.

No Market Intelligence

Companies have no visibility into what skills are in demand, what salary benchmarks look like, or how their hiring compares to the market.

The Solution

What OPTIOHIRE Does

An AI hiring agent that processes every applicant, extracts skills, matches against role requirements, and ranks by market demand data.

AI Skill Extraction

Scans every CV, extracts technical and soft skills, maps them to standardised taxonomies, and builds a competency profile for each candidate, regardless of CV format.

Market-Demand Ranking

Ranks candidates not just by skill match, but by how in-demand those skills are in the current market, giving employers a talent supply-and-demand intelligence layer.

Bias-Free Shortlisting

Removes names, photos, and demographic markers from the screening process. Candidates are ranked purely on skills, experience, and market relevance.

Employer Dashboard

Real-time dashboard showing pipeline health, skill gaps, time-to-hire, and market benchmarks. Employers see where their hiring is strong and where they need to adjust.
