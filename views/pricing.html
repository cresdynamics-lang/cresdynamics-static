How we price

Built around how your company operates.

The price follows what your system has to do, not a package. Here is how we get to a number you can rely on.

1. Understand your operations

A free conversation about how your company sells, stocks, approves and gets paid today, and what is costing you time or money.

2. Scope the system

We map the departments, branches, users, integrations (M-Pesa, banks, existing tools) and data to move, then agree what the first release must do.

3. Fixed proposal

You receive a written proposal with scope, timeline and price for your company. No per-seat fees, no surprise invoices.

What changes the price: the number of modules and departments, branches and users, integrations, data migration, AI and forecasting features, and the level of support after launch.

Questions

Common questions about pricing.

Why don't you publish prices?

Because no two companies operate the same way. A school, a distributor with five warehouses and a SACCO need very different systems, so any fixed price list would be wrong for most of them. We quote after we understand your operations.

How do we get a quote?

Email info@cresdynamics.com or send the enquiry form with a short description of your company and what you need. We reply within 2 hours during working hours and set up a scoping conversation.

Can payments be spread across the project?

Yes. Payment terms are agreed in the proposal and are usually tied to project milestones.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Support and maintenance after launch are scoped to your system and included in the proposal if you want them.
